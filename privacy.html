Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Timmy Gadgets ("we", "us") runs an online gadget store based in Felele, Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your email address and name when you create an account or sign in with email or Google.
  • Your delivery details (name, phone number, address, city, state and delivery notes) when you place an order.
  • The items in your order, the order total and your chosen payment method.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account secure.
  • To process, confirm and deliver your orders and to contact you about them.
  • To answer your questions and give support.

We do not sell your personal information. We never ask you to send card PINs or one-time passwords to us.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name and email address from Google only to create and sign you in to your account. We do not receive your Google password.

Where your data is stored

Account and order information is stored with our service provider Supabase. The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers keep data secure on our behalf.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us.
